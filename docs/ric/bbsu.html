Biostatistics and Bioinformatics Support Unit

Part of the Research Capacity Core (RCC), providing statistical design, analysis, and bioinformatics support for research projects.

Located in the Portage Building on Cold Spring Lane, the BBSU provides space, software, computers, and research statistical analysis services to Morgan students and faculty.

Computer Lab

The Portage building has a computer lab with 22 Dell desktop computers. Installed statistical packages include:

  • SAS 9.4
  • STATA 14.2
  • SPSS 24.0
  • ATLAS.ti 8.0 (qualitative data analysis software)

Services

  • Provide assistance in designing the analytical portion of studies.
  • Provide assistance with data analysis.
  • Provide assistance with sample size determination of studies.
  • Conduct Summer Institute on biostatistical methods, data analyses, and bioinformatics.
  • Conduct training on R statistical software.
  • Conduct training on STATA statistical software.
