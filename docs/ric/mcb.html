Molecular & Cellular Biology Core

Part of the Research Capacity Core (RCC), providing shared instrumentation, expertise, and support for molecular and cellular biology research.

The Morgan State University Molecular & Cellular Biology Core Laboratory (MCB) is a shared instrumentation laboratory that provides a comprehensive range of services to support modern experimental biomedical research. These include advanced equipment, technical assistance, training, and supplies to initiate research in the early years of implementation. The MCB is a state-of-the-art facility powered by a diverse team of qualified professionals focused on sequencing technology, flow cytometry, confocal microscopy, and molecular characterization, backed by a thorough array of laboratory support equipment. The facility covers several floors of the Richard N. Dixon Science Research Center to provide space for dedicated rooms, supplies, and specialized equipment to accommodate the needs of our users.

Equipment
🔬

The MCB provides access to a wide array of equipment for biomedical research, focused on flow cytometry, genomic analysis, biological assays, biomedical imaging, nanomaterials research, molecular separation and identification, and histology. Tap a category below to see what's available.

🧬 Flow Cytometry 2 items
  1. SONY SH800 cell sorter with the capacity to sort different cell sizes using microfluidics sorting chips. The sorter is equipped with 488nm/638nm/408nm lasers.
  2. Cytek Aurora Spectral analyzer equipped with 405nm, 488nm, and 640nm lasers. The spectral unmixing technology allows for the efficient separation of spectra from different fluorochromes. As set up, 38 color phenotyping can be accomplished from a single experiment. This system provides high sensitivity, flexible multicolor panel design, and robust resolution of complex and overlapping fluorochromes, supporting advanced applications such as deep immunophenotyping, rare-cell detection, and high-parameter flow cytometry experiments.

  4. QuantStudio™ 6 Flex: Real-Time PCR System, 96 or 384-well, with laptop.
  5. Nanovue Plus & Nanodrop spectrophotometers, 16-well Take3 plate for spectrophotometry on the H1 Synergy microplate reader, and a Qubit 4 Fluorometer.

  2. Xevo TQ-GC Mass Spectrometry System is a robust Electron Ionization (EI) and Chemical Ionization (CI) Gas Chromatography (GC)-MS/MS platform that allows routine quantification of multiple contaminants in complex matrices.
  3. Flashsmart Elemental Analyzer for quantitative determination of nitrogen, carbon, hydrogen, sulfur and oxygen. Flexible handling of any sample type: solids, liquid, viscous, volatile and gas.

  5. Agilent 6546 Q-TOF LC/MS System with Agilent Jet Stream source, oil foreline pump, bundled PC, monitor, MassHunter software suite – at the low and high concentrations with simultaneous delivery of five orders of in-spectrum dynamic range and mass resolution over 60k (for high masses) and over 30k (for low masses).

  7. Fluorolog-QM: enables steady-state and time-resolved luminescence measurements, including time-correlated single photon counting (TCSPC) and photoluminescence quantum yield (PLQY) measurements. All-reflective optics, resulting in exceptional focus across its entire wavelength range (180 nm to 5500 nm).

Service Subscription

📱

Researchers schedule and reserve MCB services using iLab (my.ilabsolutions.com) via Quick Response (QR) codes conveniently located on-site by each room or instrument. Access is granted after an initial consultation for each service, to ensure that your specific needs and objectives are addressed effectively.

Internal (Morgan State) users are billed through the tiered Subscription plan below, based on how much they plan to use the suite of resources available at the core lab — this makes it easy for grant applicants to simply fill out a line item to declare their projected costs for institutional core support. Labs with no current active or eligible grant are subsidized at no cost. External or Industry researchers are charged à la carte per service, as detailed below. Prices are subject to change in line with current needs, availability, and consensus with other institutional cores.

Other Services

💬

Initial Consultation

We provide a free initial consultation to discuss the specific needs of the researchers. This allows us to provide tailored recommendations for the effective use of our laboratory resources. This consultation is initiated and tracked in iLab.

🎓

Methods Training

We provide training in the equipment offered by the core laboratory, to the extent of current faculty staff expertise. Training is scheduled and tracked in iLab and guided by training workflows to ensure a standard and acceptable level of proficiency.

🧪

Sample Pooling

Equipment that may efficiently run samples from multiple researchers at once, such as the MiSeq and Lyophilizer, are scheduled and allotted in iLab.

📜

Protocol Development

We provide assistance in developing new protocols for investigators planning to use our resources. This service includes specific and detailed methods for equipment and services as well as advice on budgeting. Protocol development is initiated and tracked in iLab.

📊

Statistical Consulting

We provide advice and assistance in statistical analysis of data, including bioinformatics analysis and figure formatting. Statistical software such as R, SPSS, MATLAB, and Graphpad Prism are available for use and training.

🖻️

Image Analysis

We provide advice and assistance for imaging analysis of biological images, including ImageQuant TL, ImageJ/FIJI, MATLAB, CellProfiler, and more.
