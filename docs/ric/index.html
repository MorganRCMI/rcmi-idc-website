Research Capacity Core

The Research Capacity Core (RCC) coordinates three critical components of ongoing MSU research activity:

  • Molecular & Cellular Biology Core (MCB)
  • Animal Research Facility (ARF)
  • Biostatistics and Bioinformatics Support Unit (BBSU)

The Goal of the RCC is to provide researchers with vital support, expertise, and instrumentation to ensure the success of their research projects.

Each component of the RCC is managed by its own Director to ensure that services are tailored specifically to the needs of the users.

Scheduling of instruments, software, training, and billing is handled by a central booking system (iLab) and well-integrated with a suite of other institutions using the same system.

As part of a wider scientific community, the RCC welcomes collaboration with industry and other academic institutions.
