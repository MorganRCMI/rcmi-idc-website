Morgan CARES

How to join our network of community and academic stakeholders working together on issues that directly impact our communities.

Join the Morgan CARES Network

The Morgan CARES Network includes close to 500 community members, organizations, faculty members, students, and leaders. This is a vibrant ecosystem of individuals interested in community health and committed to collaboration. If you would like to join the network, fill out the Network Registration Form. Once you receive the confirmation message, follow the instructions listed to complete a profile.

PartnerLink

As a Network member, you can also opt into PartnerLink. A primary goal of our work at Morgan CARES is to facilitate new connections and support community-campus partnerships that promote health in Baltimore City. Morgan CARES provides linkage support to help potential collaborators connect with one another. With matching software, the team can use the profile information provided to introduce individuals with complementary skills and interests.

If you are looking to develop a new partnership, join PartnerLink. Want to get started? Check out the instructional video to learn more.

Network Membership vs. Being a Partner of Morgan CARES vs. Joining a Collaborative Partnership

Network Membership means that you will be affiliated with Morgan CARES and participate in the exchange of information and resources. Members receive announcements, newsletters, and opportunities for training and capacity-building. Some organizations are Partners of Morgan CARES. That involves a long-term collaboration with an established Memorandum of Understanding. Organizational staff and Morgan CARES staff work together on toward agreed upon goals and on shared activities. Joining a Collaborative Partnership refers to instances where individuals or organizations work with one another. Morgan CARES has seed funding programs that provide resources to support these kinds of partnerships. Collaborators that would like to propose a health-related initiative that would positively impact Baltimore communities can apply to either the Community Award Program or Practitioner Research Award. Check out at our Seed Funding Programs page to learn more. At any level you choose, Morgan CARES offers programming to facilitate relationship-building, bi-directional learning, and community-based participatory research.
