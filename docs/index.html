About the Center

One Center, Six Cores

RCMI@Morgan is led by Principal Investigator Dr. Paul B. Tchounwou, Dean of the School of Computer, Mathematical and Natural Sciences, under the oversight of Morgan State University's Office of the President and the Office of Research and Economic Development.

The Center is organized into six Cores that together build research capacity, develop investigators, engage the community, and carry out funded research projects addressing urban health disparities.
