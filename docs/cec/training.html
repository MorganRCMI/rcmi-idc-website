Morgan CARES Training Initiatives

Community-engaged research prioritizes capacity-building, co-learning, skill sharing, and dialogue. Morgan CARES hosts trainings, workshops, and other learning opportunities. This helps enhance the ability of community members and academics to collaborate and generate community-driven solutions to pressing community health needs.

To achieve this, Morgan CARES offers structured workshops and specialized training programs designed to bridge the gap between academic research and grassroots initiatives. By tying mandatory capacity-building directly to financial awards, Morgan CARES training programs ensure that collaborative teams possess the strong management and research literacy needed to co-lead public health research projects. Ultimately, these training opportunities help partners to translate shared knowledge into sustainable, community-driven solutions that directly improve health outcomes in Baltimore.

Monthly Training Series

The Morgan CARES Monthly Training Series is designed to support essential skill development and resource sharing for individuals interested in community-engaged research. The series includes a wide range of topics and provides opportunities for collaboration, networking, partnership development, and sharing lessons.

Session topics include:

Participatory Research Design Research Methods Budget Partnership and Proposal Development

Partners in Research: A CBPR Training for Academic and Community Partners

Morgan CARES is developing an online, self-paced training program entitled “Partners in Research: A CBPR Training for Academic and Community Partners”.

It presents material about community-based participatory research principles, ethics, practices for developing equitable partnerships, and much more! Utilizing real-world examples and engaging activities, participants will gain valuable skills and knowledge. It is designed for a diverse audience, including community members and community-based organizations, faculty, students, researchers, and professionals working in health-related and practice-based disciplines.

Whether you are new to CBPR or have engaged in research partnerships for many years, you can build the confidence and capacity to meaningfully engage in community-campus collaborations and to embed community engagement principles throughout your work. If you are interested, join the Morgan CARES network to receive updates. The program will launch in Fall 2026!
