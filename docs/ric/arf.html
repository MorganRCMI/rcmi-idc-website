Animal Research Facility

Part of the Research Capacity Core (RCC), supporting animal-model research with dedicated facilities, husbandry, and technical expertise.

The Animal Research Facility (ARF) was built in 2017 and includes a state-of-the-art vivarium and research facility. This research facility was built, staffed, and equipped according to the standards of the American Association for Accreditation of Laboratory Animal Care (AAALAC). Faculty and students can perform laboratory animal studies in an innovative, ethical, and humane manner. Housing is available for rats and mice and aquarium space for crayfish is included.

The Facility Manager, Caretaker, and Veterinarian are American Association for Laboratory Animal Science (AALAS) certified. All work performed in the facility is approved by the Morgan State University Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee and adheres to Occupational Health and Safety guidelines. Any interested investigator is encouraged to access policies and procedures on the resources and training drive.

The ARF maintains compliance at all times with federal Animal Welfare Act requirements for laboratory animal care and use.

Equipment and Apparatus

🐇

The ARF houses dedicated surgical, histology, molecular, and behavioral testing spaces, backed by a full suite of husbandry and environmental support equipment.

🧞

Surgery Room

State-of-the-art surgery room with two surgery tables (one backdraft and one downdraft), digitized stereotaxic instruments, gas and chemical anesthesia system, animal physiological monitoring (Kent Scientific), and autoclave system for surgical sterilization.

🔬

Histology Laboratory

Biosafety level II cabinet, chemical fume hood for perfusions, HistoCore Arcadia tissue processing and embedding apparatus, and a Leica RM2255 fully automated rotary microtome.

🧬

Molecular Laboratory

Hacker Bright cryostat, a Keyence BZ-XB fluorescence microscope with live-cell imaging and automated cell counting, refrigerated centrifuge, digital temperature shaking incubator, Wes™ Simple Western blot protein analysis apparatus, a microplate reader, and various small laboratory equipment items.

🎯

Behavior Testing Rooms

Three rooms with automated operant conditioning chambers for fear conditioning and drug self-administration; light-dark boxes; modular mazes; a Rotarod for mice and rats with video recording; manual open-field, Elevated Plus, and T-Maze for mice and rats; and a T-maze for small aquatic species.

💧

Bedding & Water Station

Automated bedding dispenser and water filling station.

🪥

Cage Washer

Cabinet cage washer with separate clean and dirty sides.

🚿

Water Purification

Reverse osmosis purification water system.

❄️

Dry Ice Maker

On-site dry ice production for sample handling and transport.

🌡️

Environmental Monitoring

Automated environmental monitoring system with 24-hour monitoring for rodent circadian rhythm studies, with environmental temperature and humidity controls.

🪧

Cold Storage

Three freezers (including an ultra-low temperature freezer) and 2 refrigerators for sample storage.
