Pilot Grant Program

The IDC offers competitive pilot grant funding for innovative research projects that address health disparities and advance biomedical knowledge.

Eligibility Requirements:

  • → Faculty or researchers affiliated with Morgan State University
  • → Focus on health disparities research
  • → Early-stage investigators encouraged to apply

Support Available:

  • → Grant funding for research projects
  • → Writing and research support
  • → Mentorship and guidance
  • → Research methodology consultation

Contact us today to learn more about application deadlines and requirements.
