Morgan CARES Training Initiatives

Community-engaged research prioritizes capacity-building, co-learning, skill sharing, and dialogue. Morgan CARES hosts trainings, workshops, and other learning opportunities. This helps enhance the ability of community members and academics to collaborate and generate community-driven solutions to pressing community health needs.

To achieve this, Morgan CARES offers structured workshops and specialized training programs designed to bridge the gap between academic research and grassroots initiatives. Ultimately, these training opportunities help partners translate shared knowledge into sustainable, community-driven solutions that directly improve health outcomes in Baltimore.

By tying mandatory capacity-building directly to financial awards, we make sure every collaborative team has the management and research literacy to co-lead a public health project — not just take part in one.

Partners in Research: A CBPR Training for Academic and Community Partners

Morgan CARES is developing an online, self-paced training program entitled “Partners in Research: A CBPR Training for Academic and Community Partners”.

It presents material about community-based participatory research principles, ethics, practices for developing equitable partnerships, and much more. Using real-world examples and engaging activities, participants gain valuable skills and knowledge — whether they are new to CBPR or have partnered on research for years.

Designed for a diverse audience:

Community members & organizations Faculty Students Researchers Health & practice professionals

Whether you are new to CBPR or have engaged in research partnerships for many years, you can build the confidence and capacity to meaningfully engage in community-campus collaborations and to embed community engagement principles throughout your work. If you are interested, join the Morgan CARES network to receive updates. The program will launch in Fall 2026!
