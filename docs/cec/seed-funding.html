Funding opportunities for community-academic partnerships pursuing Community-Based Participatory Research.

Overview

Community-academic research partnerships using Community Based Participatory Research (CBPR) principles are essential for addressing health disparities. CBPR emphasizes equitable partnerships with communities, recognizing strengths and resources, treating them as a unit of identity. Successful CBPR entails building trust, balancing power, negotiating roles, and promoting shared governance. These partnerships create local ecosystems for networking, resource sharing, capacity building and mentorship. Engaging diverse leaders fosters co-learning and knowledge-sharing.

With this in mind, Morgan CARES offers two seed funding programs to support the formation, maintenance, and success of collaborative research partnerships. With access to the Morgan CARES infrastructure, these partnerships can have lasting impact on community health.

Community Award Program

Overview

The Morgan CARES Community Award is a small seed funding initiative intended to support community-campus collaborations. The goal of this small award is to promote and strengthen partnerships between community-based organizations and groups, and members of academia at Morgan State University. It is our hope that these connections form a strong foundation that supports the emergence of responsive, community-led public health initiatives that promote health equity in communities across Baltimore City. New partners are encouraged to participate in Morgan CARES training, consultations, networking opportunities, and other supportive activities for as long as needed before developing and submitting an application. Existing community-campus partnerships are also eligible to apply.

Funding for new partnerships is offered every other year. During each funding cycle, Morgan CARES provides up to five (5) Community Awards of $2,000 to support the development and implementation of collaborative partnership activities. Project teams that successfully complete their initial $2,000 award and remain in good standing and actively engaged in their partnership are eligible to apply for a competitive $5,000 Community Award Continuation to further expand or build upon their work. Morgan CARES is able to fund up to two (2) continuation awards during each funding cycle.

Application Process

All applicants must have a Community Co-Lead and an Academic Co-Lead. The Academic Co-Lead must be a Faculty member or Doctoral-level student from Morgan State University in order to be eligible.

Applications are reviewed each quarter and meritorious projects are selected for funding. Applications will continue to be accepted while funds remain available.
